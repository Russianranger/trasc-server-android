# Launcher themes — 0.6.14

Necromancer now uses black and charcoal surfaces, deep crimson buttons and borders, ivory text, and four original gritty fantasy background paintings. The theme selector shares the World profile bar. On narrow displays the controls wrap inside that same bar, preserving touch-sized targets.

Choose **Launcher theme → Necromancer** beside **World profile**. Open the tabs to see the different scenes, reload the launcher, and switch world profiles to check persistence. Choose **Default** to return to the original appearance, or **Monk** for the existing jade/gold theme. The choice is local to the launcher.

The four project-bound artworks are saved at:

- `app/src/main/assets/ui/theme-necromancer-crypt.webp`
- `app/src/main/assets/ui/theme-necromancer-ritual.webp`
- `app/src/main/assets/ui/theme-necromancer-grimoire.webp`
- `app/src/main/assets/ui/theme-necromancer-ossuary.webp`

All images are bundled for offline use. They were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and encoded as WebP for the APK without changing the artwork. The former purple/green Necromancer palette and common vector background are no longer used by this theme. Default and Monk retain their existing art and palettes.

The compiled servers, client, Wine prefix, runtime, DirectX helpers, character UI, era settings and controller preferences are preserved. This is a launcher-only update and reuses all 21 verified native components from 0.6.13.

## Original artwork prompts

### Crypt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original wide 16:9 background painting for an EverQuest-inspired necromancer Android launcher. Gritty, atmospheric, mature dark fantasy with an old hand-painted game-art feel. Scene: a ruined stone necropolis at night, crumbling gothic arches, cracked gravestones and damp soot-covered masonry. A small ragged hooded necromancer carrying a weathered bone staff stands to the right, facing a distant dark crimson moon. Palette strictly near-black, charcoal, ash gray, muted bone ivory and very dark blood red; red ember accents. Rough brushwork, worn stone, tarnished metal, desaturated texture, believable depth. Compose landscape about 1536x864; keep the central-left area spacious and subdued for UI overlays, details at the edges; retain enough visibility that architecture can be recognized. No text, lettering, UI, border, logo or watermark. Avoid purple, neon green, glossy modern horror, bright saturated colors or gore.

### Ritual

Scene: underground ritual chamber, broken stone columns and a weathered circular summoning sigil cut into cracked black basalt, piles of old bones along the margins, heavy rusted chains, deep red embers and faint dark crimson fire illuminating drifting ash. A ruined necromantic altar in the distance. Old hand-painted game concept-art feel, gritty dry brush and scratches, ash gray and muted ivory against charcoal and near-black, strictly dark blood red as accent. Landscape about1536x864. Low contrast and spacious center/left for overlay controls; readable architectural silhouettes with details at the edges. No text, readable runes, UI, border, logo or watermark. Avoid purple, green, cartoon shapes, shiny polished surfaces or gore.
